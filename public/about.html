SLOVOBOY

SLOVOBOY is a free real-time multiplayer word game in Russian. Players take turns naming a word that starts with the last letter of the previous word, against a timer. Create a room, share its 5-digit code with friends and play from a computer or a phone.

  • Game modes: words, cities, names, countries, animals, food
  • Rare, epic, legendary and mythic words with special effects
  • Player profiles with statistics, boosts and reactions
  • Sign in with Google (we store only a hashed account identifier and your nickname)
